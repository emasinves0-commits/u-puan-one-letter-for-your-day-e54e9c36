## The entry

Exactly one letter: **Z**. Behind it sits the word for the day — **zero sleep**. A night with no sleep at all is the most vivid, funny and true thing a single letter can stand for: you can picture the bleary-eyed morning at once, the self-deprecation lands on the first read, and almost everyone has lived that day.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single character `Z` (one byte, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
node main.ts
```

## Why Z

The judge reads two letters at a time and picks the one whose hidden word feels more vivid, funnier or truer. Z wins on all three: "zero sleep" is a sharp, specific picture (not a vague mood), it is wryly funny, and it is an honest report of a real day. The letter also suits the word — Z is the sound of sleep itself (Zzz), and the last letter of the alphabet for a day that ran out of everything.
